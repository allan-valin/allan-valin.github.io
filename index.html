Zbor

Offline Android translator for talking in person with someone who speaks only Macedonian, where there is no signal. Speak or type, fix what speech recognition misheard before anything is translated, and the translation is spoken aloud — Macedonian included, which Google Translate no longer speaks. Speech recognition, translation and voice all run on the phone; a face-to-face mode turns the top half towards the person across the table. Macedonian, Portuguese, English and German.

onnxruntime-web · ONNX · Web Audio · 2026

Speech to IPA

Record a word in any language and get a narrow IPA transcription of what was said, not of what a dictionary says. Five phone recognizers (PhoneticXeus, POWSM, ZIPA, MultIPA, WhIPA) run in the browser and vote; symbols without a majority are highlighted, and tapping one plays that slice of the recording. Every model's reading sits in aligned columns above the consensus; a hand-correction editor uses the layout of the IPA chart. Exports a zip with the audio, every model's IPA, timings and a Praat TextGrid, and imports it back. Interface in eight languages. Nothing leaves the device; the models download once (about 1.3 GB for all five). Built on the phonetic-eval benchmark.

OpenSource

Drum notation

A drum notation editor for the phone browser, faster than writing on paper. Pick a note value, tap the staff at the instrument's height; heads, stems, beams and rests are drawn for you. Cells split one at a time, time signature per bar, % and repeats, drum noteheads and technique marks, and playback with a recorded kit, synth or silent with metronome. English, Portuguese and German.

Research & writing

  • A Comparative Survey and Evaluation of Language-Agnostic Speech-to-IPA Transcription Systems (2026). Seven self-supervised speech-to-IPA approaches surveyed, the public ones re-evaluated on one benchmark with PER and PFER. Removing narrow diacritics cuts PER by 9 to 22 points while PFER barely moves. Code: phonetic-eval.
  • Arbeits- und Führungstechniken im interkulturellen Vergleich: Brasilien und Deutschland (2026, German). Leadership, communication and knowledge management compared across the two working cultures.
  • Machine Learning for Particle Counting (bachelor thesis, 2025). Automatic counting of ions measured by a scintillation counter in a synchrotron: algorithmic labelling of clear cases, manual labelling of the rest, networks trained on both. Research conducted at a heavy-ion research centre; the ML code base was provided, my part was the labelling pipeline, the manual labelling and the evaluation. Cover and declaration pages removed. My two scripts: thesis-code.
  • Replaying Recorded Traffic in Network Digital Twins: Storage Granularity, Window Alignment, and Fidelity (with F. Stumpf and S. Rieger, in preparation, 2026). Storage and replay of network monitoring data in time-series databases for the DigSiViz visualisation. Code: digsiviz.
  • Virgílio Marão Gramático: Tradução de uma Paródia (undergraduate monograph in linguistics, UFPR, 2013, Portuguese). Partial Latin–Portuguese translation of the first four epitomes of Virgilius Maro Grammaticus, a 7th-century parody of Latin grammar, with a comparison to Donatus and to an Italian translation.

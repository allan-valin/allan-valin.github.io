Research experience

Seminar paper: A Comparative Survey and Evaluation of Language-Agnostic Speech-to-IPA Transcription Systems2026
Advanced Scientific Writing, Fulda University of Applied Sciences
  • Compared seven speech-to-IPA systems (Allosaurus, Wav2Vec2Phoneme, MultIPA, Allophant, ZIPA, WhIPA/STIPA, POWSM) on VoxAngeles (95 languages, 5,416 recordings)
  • Found that reducing to broad phones at scoring time lowers phone error rate by 9–22 points while barely changing feature error rate, and that published scores are not directly comparable
Research project (DigSiViz): Replaying Recorded Traffic in Network Digital Twins: Storage Granularity, Window Alignment, and Fidelity2026
Fulda University of Applied Sciences, supervised by Prof. Dr. Sebastian Rieger
  • Replaced DigSiViz's 60-second in-memory history with InfluxDB, fed by Telegraf from the existing Kafka transport, with a 12-tier down-sampling scheme so recorded network telemetry can be kept and replayed
  • Measured replay fidelity across storage granularities: peak accuracy depends on window size and alignment relative to event duration; storing only the fields that are read cut ingest by 97.9%
  • Stack: InfluxDB, Telegraf, Grafana, Kafka, Docker, Python/Flask; manuscript in preparation (see Papers)
